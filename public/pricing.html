Questions

How do I pay?

Payments run through Stripe's hosted Checkout, and you manage your card and invoices in the Stripe Customer Portal. Card details are never entered in Teacher Playground.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep Tutor Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, then your account returns to Free. Lessons already in progress are not interrupted.

What happens to my rooms if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Rooms over the Free limit are archived and readable, and come back exactly as they were when you upgrade again.

Is VAT included?

No. All prices are ex VAT. UK VAT is 20% and Stripe calculates it at checkout; business buyers can add a VAT ID so it appears on the invoice.
