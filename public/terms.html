Terms of Service

Last updated 20 August 2026

These terms cover the use of Teacher Playground, a shared whiteboard for tutoring. By creating a room or joining one, you agree to them.

Who may use the service

Rooms are created and owned by teachers and tutors, who must be adults able to enter a contract. Students may join a room of any age, but only where a teacher has invited them and any consent required by their school, parent, or guardian has been obtained. Obtaining that consent is the teacher's responsibility, not ours.

Teacher accounts

Teachers sign in with Google or Facebook through Cloudflare Access. We do not issue or hold passwords of our own. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in secure, and for everything done in rooms you own.

Students joining a room

Students join by opening a room link and entering a class PIN, choosing a display name for themselves. They do not create an account and give us no personal details beyond that name.

A PIN and a link are an invitation to ask to join, never a right to enter. Every student waits until the teacher admits them, and a teacher can remove any participant at any time.

Class PINs are your responsibility

A class PIN is six digits. It is deliberately short so a teacher can read it aloud, which also makes it guessable if it is published somewhere public. Share a PIN with your class, not with the internet. Rooms lock guest joining automatically after repeated wrong attempts, and a teacher can rotate or switch off guest joining at any time.

Anyone who knows a live PIN can reach your waiting room. Treat it as you would the code on a classroom door.

Supervision and safeguarding

Teacher Playground provides tools — a waiting room, per-participant approval, read-only roles, and removal — but it does not moderate lessons. The teacher who owns a room is responsible for who is admitted, for supervising what happens on the board and in the call, and for complying with their own school's safeguarding policy.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you create on a whiteboard. You grant us only the permission needed to run the service: storing the board, syncing it between participants, restoring it after a disconnect, and deleting it under the retention periods in the Privacy Policy.

Acceptable use

The service is for teaching. Do not use it to share unlawful content, to harass or endanger anyone, to attempt to reach rooms or accounts you have not been admitted to, to guess class PINs, or to disrupt the service for others.

Voice and video are live only and are never recorded by the service. Do not record other participants without their knowledge and consent.

Availability

The service is provided as-is, without a guarantee of uptime or of data preservation. Rooms unused for 90 days are deleted automatically. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Plans and payment

Some plans may require payment from the teacher account that owns a room. Students never pay and are never shown billing. Payment terms will be finalised before any charge is introduced; see pricing for the current proposal.

Suspension and termination

We may suspend or end access for accounts that breach these terms or misuse the service. You may stop using it and delete your rooms and account at any time from the account menu.

Changes

These terms may change as the service develops. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to the service owner via the contact details on the main site.
