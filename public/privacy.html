Privacy Policy

This policy describes what Teacher Playground stores and why, in plain language.

What we store

Account identity (from Cloudflare Access sign-in), whiteboard content, and the room records needed to run lessons: who belongs to a room, who is waiting, and each room's settings. Voice and video are transmitted live for the call and are not recorded.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertising. Lesson boards are never search-indexed and are visible only to the people a tutor has admitted to the room.

Students

Students join with a sign-in and a display name. Student data is kept to the minimum the lesson needs, and students never see billing or payment surfaces.

Retention

Boards are kept according to the room owner's plan and are deleted when their retention period lapses. Deleting a room removes its content.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be directed to the owner of the repository this service is built from.
