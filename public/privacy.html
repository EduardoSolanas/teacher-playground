Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Teacher Playground is a shared whiteboard for one-to-one and small-group tutoring. This policy explains exactly what the service stores, for how long, and why. It is written to be checked against the software rather than to be comforting.

The short version

Students give us a first name and nothing else. No email address, no account, no password, no social login. A student joins by opening a link and typing a class PIN their teacher reads out. That is the entire student sign-up, and it is deliberate: the safest way to hold a child's personal data is not to collect any.

Teachers

Teachers sign in with Google or Facebook through Cloudflare Access. We receive a verified account identifier from the sign-in provider, plus the name and email address on that account. The account identifier is the only thing used to decide what a teacher may do; the name and email are shown in the interface and are never used to grant access.

We never receive a teacher's password, and we never receive or store access tokens for Google or Facebook.

Students and guests

A student joining a room provides a display name they choose themselves. We do not ask for, and have no way to store, a student's email address, date of birth, school, or parent's details — those fields do not exist on the join form.

A guest identity is created for one room only. It cannot be used in any other room, it is never linked to a sign-in provider, and it is deleted when the room is deleted or when the guest's session expires. Guest sessions expire after 4 hours.

Students are always held in a waiting room until the teacher admits them. Nothing about a room — its board, its participants, or its settings — is visible to anyone before a teacher approves them.

What we store

Board content. The drawings, text, and images a room's participants add to the whiteboard.

Room records. Who belongs to a room and in what role, who is waiting to join, the room's name and settings, and the class PIN if guest joining is switched on.

Session records. A hashed session identifier, timestamps, and an expiry, so that signing out actually ends a session.

Security events. Failed sign-ins and rate-limit trips are logged so abuse can be spotted. Class PINs are never written to logs.

Voice and video

Voice and video are transmitted live between participants for the duration of the call. Calls are not recorded and are never stored. A participant admitted as a read-only viewer cannot broadcast camera or microphone into the room.

How long we keep things

These periods are enforced by the software, not by policy alone:

Waiting-room requests — deleted after 24 hours.
Removed participants — record kept 30 days so a removal cannot be undone by rejoining.
Class PINs — expire 12 hours after they are issued.
Guest sessions — expire after 4 hours.
Teacher sessions — expire after 30 minutes idle, 12 hours maximum.
Idle rooms — deleted after 90 days without use.
Deleted-room markers — a record that a room existed is kept for 365 days so a deleted room's link cannot be reused.

Deleting a room deletes its board content, its participant list, and its waiting queue.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share personal data. We do not show advertising. We do not profile or track users across other websites. Lesson boards are never indexed by search engines and are visible only to people a teacher has admitted.

Your rights

A signed-in teacher can export everything held about their account, and can delete their account and its rooms, from the account menu inside the app.

Because students are anonymous to us, we usually cannot identify a particular child's data on request — there is no email address or account to look them up by. In practice a student's data is removed when the teacher deletes the room, or automatically when the retention periods above elapse. A parent or school with a concern should contact the teacher who ran the lesson, who can delete the room.

Children

This service is designed to be used by children under a teacher's supervision. The data minimisation described above is the principal safeguard: we hold a self-chosen first name and nothing that identifies a child outside the lesson. Teachers are responsible for obtaining whatever consent their school or jurisdiction requires before inviting a student to a room.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the service owner via the contact details on the main site.
