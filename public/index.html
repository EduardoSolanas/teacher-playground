a.

Made for how tuition actually works

Not a video call with a whiteboard bolted on — a whiteboard that happens to have your student in it.

— One room per student

Every student keeps their own board. Last week's working is still there when the lesson starts — no screenshots, no redrawing the same diagram.

— A door you answer

Students knock and wait until you let them in. Someone who finds the link gets a waiting screen, never your lesson.

— Talk while you draw

Voice and video live inside the room, so you explain out loud with your pen on the working — no second app, no screen juggling.

— Nothing to install

A link and a sign-in, in any browser, on any laptop or tablet. That is the whole setup — for you and for them.
